AI-Consensus

A council of models instead of a single answer

PHP 8.4 web app that runs a council of LLMs over a BotHub-compatible API, scores their answers with a structured consensus engine and shows a clickable DAG of every deliberation step.

What it does

  • Parallel multi-model, multi-role council sessions
  • SKILLS: declarative JSON workflows with consensus rules
  • Every score and every step kept in a MySQL audit trail
